How it works
  1. GiveGive the link to a job posting. If the page cannot be read, you can upload it as a PDF. No account and no e-mail address.
  2. ReadA model lists what the posting asks for and sorts it into minimum and desirable requirements.
  3. CheckEach requirement is checked against the CV and stamped: met, partly, or not shown. The CV line behind the verdict is shown next to it.
  4. AskAsk follow-up questions. The chat answers from the posting, the CV and the report, and can argue against the match if you ask it to.
  5. DraftGet a draft cover letter in which each paragraph about the candidate cites a CV line.

What it will not do
  • Decide for you. A verdict is a model's reading of two texts, and it can be wrong. The CV line is there so you can check it.
  • Say “no”. “Not shown” means the CV does not show it, not that {{ owner_name }} cannot do it.
  • Speak for {{ owner_name }}. Software writes the report and the letter. {{ owner_name }} does not write or send them.
  • Answer questions outside the posting, the CV and the report.
  • Keep your text for long: {{ limits.retention_days }} days, or until you delete the report.

How it is built

Each step (read the posting, judge the evidence, answer, write) is a small agent built with Pydantic AI.

Every model call is recorded as an OpenTelemetry trace: the prompt, the answer, the time and the cost.

Evals check the agents against test cases with known answers, against reports the owner corrected by hand, and with a second model that judges the cited CV lines.
